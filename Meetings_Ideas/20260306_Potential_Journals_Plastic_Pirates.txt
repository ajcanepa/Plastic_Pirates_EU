################################################################
## Potential Journals for Plastic Pirates Article ##
################################################################

################################################################
Water Research (Elsevier)
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/water-research
I.F. 12.4

Strong focus on pollution, water quality, and policy/management.

Linking your results to the Water Framework Directive, plastic directives, and good ecological status objectives would greatly strengthen the fit.


################################################################
Environmental Pollution (Elsevier)
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/environmental-pollution
I.F. 7.3

Publishes extensively on plastics and litter, both marine and riverine, and studies with advanced statistical analysis of composition and sources.


################################################################
Environmental Science & Technology / ES&T Water (ACS)
https://pubs.acs.org/journal/aewcaa

I.F. 4.3

Very selective, but if you can emphasize: novelty of multinational comparison, use of validated citizen science, and direct relevance to European regulations, it could be an ambitious first choice.


################################################################
Marine Pollution Bulletin (Elsevier)
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/marine-pollution-bulletin

I.F. 4.9

Although the title is “marine,” it includes numerous studies where rivers/banks are sources of litter, often using citizen science and quantitative approaches.


################################################################
Environmental Monitoring and Assessment (Springer)
https://link.springer.com/journal/10661
I.F. 3.9

Already publishes studies on litter along riverbanks using citizen science.
